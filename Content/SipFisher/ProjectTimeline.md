### Inspiration

February

I was inspired by A Game About Digging A Hole, a minimalist incremental game that was very successful despite a small scope, and so I started planning a project like it with friends.

### MVP Prototype

March

I built an extreme MVP with just the water draining mechanic to do early exploratory playtesting and start feeling out the design space.

### Team Assembled

April

We assembled the complete Sip Fisher team at the end of my sophomore year of college from a group of friends I had previously worked with and some that my friends had worked with.

### Pre-Production

Late April

We did a week of pre-production to get a working prototype for initial playtesting, building the fishing, dock building, and shop mechanics. Meanwhile, our artists started building aesthetic with concept art.

### Production

April - June

The team charged full steam ahead to get a demo ready for Steam Next Fest, and we bring on a dedicated level designer. All the while playtesting iterations rigorously.

### Demo Release for Next Fest

Mid June

The demo launches for Next Fest and starts getting traction from some idle game YouTubers, and Steam wishlists start to build up.

### Steam Fishing Fest

Late June

We manage to scrap together a bunch of bug fixes and some more content for a demo update to participate in a Steam Fishing Game festival the next week, helping gain a bit more YouTube coverage.

### Production Slump

July

After burning hot for the demo release and update, production hits a bit of a slump. I was also personally dealing with some external pressure that made it difficult to work on the project.

### Final Push for Release

July - August

We managed to gain back momentum throughout July into August. Playtesting became more difficult with the game's playtime reaching 3 - 4 hours.

### Release Day

Late August

The game released on August 27 to an awesome 7k wishlists, and got hundreds of sales on the first day (blasting past our expectations from the start of the project).

### Post Release

September - Eternity

We were lucky to get a constant stream of YouTube content for months after release, pushing our wishlists and copies sold up and up.

In December 2025, some team members worked to create an update adding a new fish and steam achievements, although I was largely uninvolved due to other projects.
